**Design refinement made while implementing** (not a new open question,
a controllability decision within the confirmed weekly-generation
budget): lessons are seeded as **stubs** (title + order + level, no
body) across all three levels up front, and the weekly AI generation
job writes the body for the NEXT stub in order — rather than inventing
an entirely new topic from scratch each week. This keeps every
generated lesson on a pre-approved topic (reviewed by the Director
right now, in this outline) instead of the AI choosing its own subject
weekly, which is a meaningfully safer shape for unattended content
generation. Once all seeded stubs are filled, later runs can propose
new stub topics for review — not built in this first version.
